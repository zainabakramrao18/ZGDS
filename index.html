CASPAM
MESSAGE FROM THE DIRECTOR

Welcome to ZGDS — CASPAM Chapter

It is a pleasure to welcome you to the online home of the Zakariyan Google Developers Society (ZGDS), CASPAM Chapter. This community can help students explore technology, share knowledge, and connect mathematical thinking with practical development.

I encourage students to approach new ideas with curiosity, collaborate respectfully, and use their skills to create useful, responsible solutions. May this society become a place where learning is shared, creativity is encouraged, and promising student projects begin.

We look forward to seeing the community grow through thoughtful events, peer learning and meaningful projects.
